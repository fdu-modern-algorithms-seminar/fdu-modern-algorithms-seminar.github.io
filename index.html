Fudan Modern Algorithms Seminar

School of Mathematical Sciences, Fudan University

It is a seminar on Modern Algorithms, organized under the School of Mathematical Sciences at Fudan University.

Syllabus 26fall

  1. Opening Lecture & Compression Algorithms — Yuhan Chi
  2. Probabilistic Data Structures
  3. Clustering Algorithms
  4. High-Dimensional Algorithms: LSH & ANN
  5. Memory Hierarchy & Cache-Aware Algorithms
  6. Parallel, GPU & Async Algorithms
  7. Optimization Algorithms: GD to Modern Methods
  8. Randomized Optimization: SA & Beyond
  9. Online Algorithms & Online Learning
  10. Fast Polynomial Algorithms: FFT, Convolution & Generating Functions
  11. Quantum Computation: Qubit, Grover & Fault Tolerance

This seminar is launched by Yuhan Chi. Great thanks to all participants!
